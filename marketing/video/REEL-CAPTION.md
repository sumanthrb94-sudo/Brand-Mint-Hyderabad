# The 30s brand film — caption, alt text and posting notes

The film is `marketing/video/out/brandmint-reel-30s-subs.mp4`
(1080x1920 · 30.00s · H.264 · AAC · karaoke subs burned in).

Same caption rules as the carousels in `marketing/social/CAPTIONS.md`, and for
the same reasons: Instagram hard-capped hashtags at five on 19 Dec 2025, so a
sixth is not a style problem — the post does not go out as written. A question
and an explicit comment CTA are the two cheapest measured effects available.
"Hyderabad" goes in the first line because the first line is what shows before
"more", and this business is only sold to one city.

## Posting notes

**Add music in the Instagram composer, not in the file.** The film ships as
voice over silence on purpose: music added in the composer is licensed for the
platform, is credited, gets the reel into that track's feed, and a viewer can
mute it. Music burned into the upload is none of those things and is a takedown
risk on a commercial post.

**Do not add Instagram's own captions on top.** The subtitles are burned in and
already sit above the 35% bottom safe zone. Instagram's auto-captions would
double them up and land lower.

**The last 4.65s is the end card**, holding the URL and the WhatsApp number
while the reel loops. Do not trim the tail to "tighten" it — that is the only
part of the film carrying a way to get in touch.

# The all-Veo cut — `brandmint-veo-30s-subs.mp4`

Same voiceover and same subtitles as the film above; the difference is beat 3.
The screen cut shows the actual site on a phone, this one shows the shop
floor. **Do not post them on the same day with the same caption** — to anyone
who saw the first, a near-identical caption on near-identical footage reads as
a repost. This caption leads with the story rather than the offer, so the two
posts earn their own place.
