# The three clip prompts

Three clips, every film, at three points, doing three different jobs. Generate
them fresh for each script — the *slots* are fixed, the *subjects* change.

## The constant

Ends every prompt, unchanged:

> 1080×1920 vertical, 5 seconds, no people, no faces, no hands, no text, no
> logos, no readable screens.

And this palette line, so the three cut together and match the graphics:

> muted desaturated palette, deep forest green and warm kraft brown, soft
> overcast daylight from one side, shallow depth of field, subtle film grain,
> no text or branding anywhere

State the camera move explicitly. *Slow push in · drift sideways · pull back ·
handheld sway · locked off.* "Cinematic" is not a camera move.

## Slot A — the world · lands 15–25%

Establishes where the problem lives. Still and observational. One subject, alone
in the frame, lit from one side. Nothing happens yet.

> A single [SUBJECT] on a [SURFACE], alone in the frame. Slow push in. One shaft
> of soft daylight across the surface, everything else in shadow. Calm, still,
> slightly ominous. <palette> <constant>

## Slot B — the turn · lands 45–60%

The consequence. This is the only clip with real movement — motion blur, sway,
something leaving or being carried away. It should feel unresolved.

> [SUBJECT] [IN MOTION / BEING TAKEN AWAY], handheld feel with slight sway,
> motion blur through the frame, the background receding. Unresolved.
> <palette> <constant>

## Slot C — the resolution · lands 75–90%

Must feel visually **opposite to slot A**: warmer, ordered, more light, more
depth. That contrast is the argument the film is making.

> A clean ordered [SETTING]: [SUBJECT] alongside [TWO SMALL PROPS], warm and
> calm. Slow push in. Soft even light, no harsh shadows. The opposite of the
> earlier shots. <palette> <constant>

## Worked example — the RTO film

**A · the sale**
> A single kraft cardboard parcel sealed with plain paper tape, sitting alone on
> a pale concrete table. Slow push in toward the parcel. One shaft of soft
> daylight across the surface, everything else in shadow. Calm, still, slightly
> ominous. Muted desaturated palette, deep forest green and warm kraft brown,
> soft overcast daylight from one side, shallow depth of field, subtle film
> grain, no text or branding anywhere. 1080×1920 vertical, 5 seconds, no people,
> no faces, no hands, no text, no logos, no readable screens.

**B · the journey back**
> Interior of a delivery van with the back doors open, a few kraft parcels
> sliding gently as the vehicle moves, road receding away through the doors.
> Handheld feel, slight sway, motion blur. The parcels are going the wrong way.
> <palette> <constant>

**C · the fix**
> A clean modern desk: a smartphone lying face up showing an abstract interface
> with soft green accents, beside a small potted plant and a closed notebook.
> Warm, ordered, calm. Slow push in on the phone. <palette> <constant>

## If a clip comes back unusable

Build that scene as animation instead and say which one in the delivery note.
Never stretch the other two to cover the gap — the rhythm is the point.
