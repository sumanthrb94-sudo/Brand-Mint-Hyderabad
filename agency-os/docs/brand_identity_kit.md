# Brand Mint Studios — Brand Identity Kit

## 1. Identity premise

**Brand Mint Studios** is a Hyderabad-rooted custom software and web agency with a global delivery posture. The identity should feel composed, exact, and human: it combines a warm editorial base with a sharp mint signal. This direction draws on the existing public-interface reference and the connected Instagram profile, which positions the studio around custom software, web delivery, and the promise, **“We build. You scale.”**

> This is a proposed operational identity system. It should be treated as the approved working direction only after the owner signs off on the recreated symbol and wordmark.

## 2. Logo system

| Element | Definition | Primary use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Symbol** | A bespoke uppercase **M** in deep forest green, placed inside a bright mint circle. | Favicon, Instagram avatar, app icon, mobile navigation. |
| **Primary lockup** | Symbol followed by the `BRAND MINT` wordmark. | Website header, proposal covers, social graphics. |
| **Location lockup** | Primary lockup followed by a fine divider and `HYDERABAD`. | Website header, profile banners, local collateral. |
| **Reverse lockup** | Mint symbol and cream or mint wordmark on forest green. | Dark surfaces, video end cards, presentation covers. |

The symbol must retain a clear area equal to **one-quarter of the symbol diameter** on every side. The circular mark must never be stretched, rotated, outlined, filled with a photograph, or used with a competing decorative icon.

## 3. Colour system

| Name | Hex | Role |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Brand Mint** | `#9DFFC3` | Signal colour, circular mark, focused CTA and selected states. |
| **Forest Ink** | `#113F32` | Primary wordmark, headlines, buttons and dark fields. |
| **Paper Cream** | `#F6F0E6` | Main background and calm editorial space. |
| **Soft Sage** | `#DCEAD9` | Quiet panel background and secondary surface. |
| **Moss Grey** | `#657469` | Supporting copy, metadata and dividers. |
| **Charcoal** | `#1B2420` | High-contrast body text where Forest Ink is not used. |

Use **Paper Cream** as the dominant field, **Forest Ink** for structure, and **Brand Mint** as an intentional signal rather than a background wash. Maintain readable contrast for all text and interactive states.

## 4. Typography direction

| Role | Direction | Recommended web font |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Display** | Elegant high-contrast editorial serif, confident and concise. | `Cormorant Garamond` or `DM Serif Display` |
| **Wordmark and navigation** | Monospaced or technical grotesk in uppercase with tracking. | `DM Mono` |
| **Body** | Contemporary neutral sans, generously spaced. | `Manrope` or `Inter` |
| **Data and labels** | Compact monospaced utility text. | `DM Mono` |

Use sentence case for explanatory writing. Reserve all caps for short navigation labels, sectional references, and the wordmark. The visual hierarchy should come from scale and whitespace rather than excessive bolding.

## 5. Visual language

The visual environment should use warm cream backgrounds, forest-green fields, fine sage dividers, rounded yet restrained geometric surfaces, and a single strong mint signal. Photography, when used, should be documentary and work-focused rather than generic startup imagery. Interfaces should feel engineered: meaningful space, crisp type, and a clear conversion path.

## 6. Instagram application

Use the **symbol-only avatar** at all social-profile sizes. Feed assets should begin with a calm Paper Cream or Forest Ink field, use the M symbol sparingly in a corner or closing frame, and maintain a consistent mix of editorial type, product/interface crops, and short technical claims. Do not place the full horizontal lockup inside a circular profile image.
