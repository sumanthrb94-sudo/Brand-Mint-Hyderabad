07 — FAQ

Common questions.

How is a project priced?

Fixed scope, fixed price, in INR, agreed in writing before anything starts. GST is extra at 18%. Payment is half to start and half on completion — completion means finished, tested and signed off on staging, and go-live follows payment.

How long does a build take?

A typical website build is three to five weeks. Internal tools and stores depend on scope — you get a date with the quote, not after it.

What happens if I want changes?

Two review rounds are included in every engagement. Beyond that, further rounds are quoted up front so nothing is billed by surprise.

What if something breaks after launch?

Anything not built to spec is fixed free for 30 days. Beyond that, a care retainer covers hosting, monitoring and small changes.

Who actually does the work?

A partner leads every engagement and signs off every shipment. Specialists are brought in per project, and you are told who is on yours before it starts.

Do you work with clients outside India?

Yes. We have shipped for teams in the UK and elsewhere. Pricing is quoted in INR and GST applies where relevant.
