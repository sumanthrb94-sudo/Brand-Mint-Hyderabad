## Test as a customer

☐ Open the homepage — does it look right?
☐ Find a product using search
☐ Leave a product review, and add one to your wishlist *(Commerce)*
☐ Filter by category
☐ Open a product — is every detail correct?
☐ Add to cart, change quantity, remove an item
☐ Create an account with your phone number, get the OTP
☐ Log out and log back in
☐ Place an order with online payment — **use a real card for a small amount**
☐ Place an order with Cash on Delivery
☐ Check the confirmation email arrived
☐ Apply a discount code at checkout *(Growth+)*
☐ Find the order in your account
☐ Request a return *(Growth+)*

## Test as the shop owner

☐ Log into the admin dashboard
☐ Log in as a staff member and confirm they see only what they should *(Commerce)*
☐ Find the orders you just placed
☐ Change an order's status
☐ Add a product with photos and price
☐ Edit a product
☐ Set a product out of stock, then check it shows as unavailable to customers
☐ Check the low-stock alert *(Growth+)*
☐ Open the sales report *(Growth+)*, and export it *(Commerce)*
☐ Process the return you requested *(Growth+)*
☐ Open the support inbox *(Growth+)*
☐ Download a GST invoice — your store issues these to your customers, under your GSTIN

## Test the edges

☐ Try to checkout with an empty cart
☐ Enter a wrong OTP
☐ Enter a wrong pincode
☐ Order more than the stock available
☐ Open the site on a different phone

## Two kinds of feedback

**A defect** — something doesn't work the way the scope says. We fix it free.

**A new idea** — something the scope doesn't describe. Very welcome, and we'll quote it. It doesn't hold up your launch.

Both are fine. We'll tell you which is which when you report it.
