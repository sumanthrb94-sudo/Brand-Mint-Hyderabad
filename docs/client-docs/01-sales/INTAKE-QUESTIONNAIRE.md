# Client Intake Questionnaire

**Send this before you quote.** It costs the client ten minutes and saves you from quoting blind. If a lead won't fill it in, they will not respect your timeline either.

---

## Business

1. Business name (as it should appear on the site)
2. Registered legal name and GST number
3. Website or social profiles you currently run
4. What do you sell, in one sentence?
5. Who buys it? Describe your typical customer.

## Catalogue

6. How many products at launch?
7. Do products have variants — size, colour, weight? Roughly how many per product?
8. Do you have product photographs already? Who took them?
9. Do you have product descriptions written?
10. Do you expect the catalogue to grow past 200 products in year one?

## Selling

11. Payment methods needed — online, Cash on Delivery, or both?
12. Do you already have a Razorpay account? Is it activated?
13. Do you ship India-wide or to specific regions?
14. Which courier do you use, or plan to use?
15. Do you sell anywhere else — Amazon, Flipkart, Instagram, a physical shop?
16. Do you need discount codes or offers at launch?

## Operations

17. Who will manage orders day to day?
18. How comfortable is that person with software, honestly?
19. Do you currently track stock anywhere?
20. How do customers reach you now for support?

## Brand

21. Do you have a logo? In what file format?
22. Brand colours and fonts, if fixed?
23. Two or three websites you like, and what you like about each.
24. Anything you strongly dislike in a website?

## Project

25. Is there a date this needs to be live by, and what happens on that date?
26. Who signs off decisions? Is that one person or several?
27. What budget range are you working with?
28. Has anyone built this for you before? What went wrong?

## Before we start, we will need from you

- Product photography
- Brand assets — logo, colours, fonts (if existing)
- Product names, descriptions and prices
- Product data entry — we set up the structure and load a sample set
- Razorpay account activated in your name — add hello@brandmintstudios.in as a team member on your own account
- Domain access — add hello@brandmintstudios.in to your registrar, or tell us to register one for you
- Business and GST details for invoicing
- Legal policy sign-off — Privacy, Terms, Refund, Shipping
- Testing and sign-off within the 5-day UAT window

**Timelines begin when the deposit clears and these items are received.** Delays in providing them extend the delivery date day for day.
