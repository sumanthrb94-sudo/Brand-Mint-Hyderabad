## F · Foundations to build on

Researched for licence as much as for stars, because you intend to **resell**
what you build on these. Star counts confirmed live where marked ✓.

| Tool | Stars | Licence | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| **shadcn/ui** | 119,930 ✓ | **MIT** | **Use it.** Copy-paste components — you own the code, it is not a dependency. |
| **Motion** (framer-motion) | 32,976 ✓ | MIT | Use it. Animation for client sites. |
| n8n | 198,222 ✓ | **Sustainable Use** | Self-host for your own and client work — but you **may not sell n8n-as-a-service**. Check before building an offering on it. |
| Dify | 150,429 ✓ | Apache + conditions | Same shape — **no multi-tenant SaaS** without permission. |
| Remotion | 54,467 ✓ | **Free ≤3 people** | Fine while you are solo. **A paid company licence is required as you grow.** Plan for it. |

> **The three fair-code tools are the trap.** n8n, Dify and Remotion are
> excellent and free *today, at your size*. The moment you resell them as a
> service or pass three people, they are licensed products. Build the client
> deliverable on MIT foundations and use the others as your own internal
> tooling.

### Where the UI kit goes — and where it does not

**shadcn/ui belongs in `commerce-skeleton`.** That is the client-build starter;
it is React, it has a build step, and beautiful components are exactly what it
needs.

**It must not go into this repository.** The Brand Mint portal is static HTML
with no build step, no `package.json`, and zero runtime dependencies — that is
what lets one person maintain it and what makes it survive two weeks of being
ignored. Adding React and Tailwind here means a build pipeline, a dependency
tree to patch, and a site that breaks in ways you cannot debug on a phone. The
constraint is load-bearing, not laziness.

On "Claude is bad at UI" — partly fair. What the portal has instead is
measured contrast and honest empty states, which were the two things that had
already shipped broken. For client work, shadcn is the right answer and I will
wire it into the skeleton on your word.

2. **Move Internal tools to the featured slot.** Best margin, buried third.
3. **Publish the retainer ladder.** You have no recurring product on the site

   consuming the capacity you need for sales.
4. **Sell four Growth retainers.** That is break-even. Everything after is
   profit.
