20-point pass

Launch-readiness checklist

Every Brand Mint store clears these twenty checks before it goes live. Run them against a store you already have. Anything unticked is losing you orders or trust, usually both.

01Speed and mobile

  • Lighthouse 90+ on mobile, all four axesPerformance, Accessibility, Best Practices, SEO. Test the home page, a product page and the cart. Chrome → DevTools → Lighthouse, mobile preset.
  • Largest Contentful Paint under 2.5 seconds on 4GUsually the hero image. Serve WebP or AVIF, sized to the slot, and preload it.
  • Images lazy-loaded below the foldEvery product image outside the first screen carries loading="lazy".
  • Tested on a real Android and a real iPhoneNot the emulator. Tap targets, sticky bars, keyboard on the checkout form, the back button.
  • No horizontal scroll at 320px wideShrink the browser to a small phone. Nothing should push the page sideways.

02Commerce, end to end

  • A real Razorpay order, start to finishPlace a ₹1 order with a live card or UPI. Confirm the order appears in admin, stock decrements, and the confirmation arrives.
  • A real cash-on-delivery orderSame path, COD. Confirm the order flags as unpaid and the confirmation step works.
  • A refund, back by the original methodRefund the ₹1 order from admin. Watch it land in Razorpay and in the customer's email.
  • Order emails and the GST invoice arriveConfirmation, shipped and delivered mails; invoice PDF with GSTIN, HSN and the right rate. Check the spam folder too.
  • A coupon applies and expires correctlyCreate a test code, use it, confirm it stops working after its end date or usage cap.

03Trust and legal

  • Terms, Privacy, Shipping, Returns and Cancellation pages existLinked from the footer and from checkout. Razorpay checks for these before enabling settlements.
  • Legal name, address, contact and grievance officer visibleConsumer Protection (E-Commerce) Rules 2020. A name, an email and a response time.
  • MRP, net quantity, manufacturer and country of origin on packaged goodsLegal Metrology rules apply to the listing, not just the pack.
  • Privacy policy matches what the store actually doesAnalytics, pixels, WhatsApp, email tools: if it runs, it's named. Marketing opt-in is separate from purchase.

04Technical

  • Domain, SSL and redirectshttp → https, www ↔ bare domain, one canonical version. Certificate auto-renews.
  • Custom 404 page and a working searchA dead link should lead somewhere useful, with a search box and the top categories.
  • Backups and uptime monitoring switched onDaily database backup you have restored at least once. An uptime monitor that messages you.

05Marketing plumbing

  • Link previews render on WhatsApp and InstagramOpen Graph title, description and a 1200×630 image on every product and the home page.
  • GA4 and Meta Pixel fire the right events with valuesview_item, add_to_cart, begin_checkout, purchase with amount and currency. Verify in DebugView and the Pixel helper.
  • Sitemap submitted, robots.txt correct, Search Console verifiedProducts and categories in the sitemap; admin and cart paths disallowed.
